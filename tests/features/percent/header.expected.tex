With a header, the percent sign is jmarkdown's comment in prose: the rest of
its line is left out of both outputs. This line has a comment and the next line still shows.

Where the comment does not reach, a caption or a theorem's note, the HTML
shows the percent sign, and so does the PDF.

\begin{figure}[htbp]
\centering
Figure body.

\caption{Caption share up 90\%}
\label{fig:pct}
\end{figure}

\begin{theorem}[Note 95\% sure]
Theorem body.
\end{theorem}

An escaped \% prints one percent.
