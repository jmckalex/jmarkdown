A bare address prints its underscores and tildes: https://example.com/a_b_c and https://example.com/~me/page, https://example.com/x_y~z in angle brackets, and www.example.com/w_x with no scheme.

A link written out keeps its URL's percent sign inside a footnote,1 in bold, the bold link, in bold italics, the other, and in a heading:

Heading linked and https://example.com/j_k

  • https://example.com/l_m~n in a list

A written-out link's underscore and tilde are as they were: text.
