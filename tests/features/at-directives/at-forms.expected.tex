The \mintinline{text}|@name[...]| inline and \mintinline{text}|@name+[...]| block forms share the begin-end
registry and coexist with the \mintinline{text}|:|\emph{\mintinline{text}|::|}\mintinline{text}|:::| directives.

Cross-reference parity (\mintinline{text}|@| must resolve identically to \mintinline{text}|:|):
ref \ref{a} then \ref{b}; cref \cref{a} then \cref{b}; Cref \Cref{a} then \Cref{b}

Span parity: <span>hello</span> then hello
