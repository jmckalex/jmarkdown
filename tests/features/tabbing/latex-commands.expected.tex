\section{LaTeX, as written}

LaTeX's own commands, as a \mintinline{text}|@begin(tabbing)| environment; cells carry inline
formatting and maths, and \mintinline{text}|\a'| / \mintinline{text}|\a=| are the accents.

\begin{tabbing}
\textbf{Symbol}  \= $x^2 + y^2$  \= \emph{the circle} \\
\textbf{Name} \> $r$ \> radius \\
Caf\a'{e} \> $\bar{x}$ \> the m\a={e}an
\end{tabbing}

The same commands inside the fence, with \mintinline{text}|\pushtabs|, \mintinline{text}|\poptabs|, \mintinline{text}|\kill| and
\mintinline{text}|\\[len]|:

\begin{tabbing}
Name:  \= Street:  \= Phone \\
Alice \> 12 Oak \> 555 \\
\pushtabs
A much longer column  \= x \kill
\> temporary \\
\poptabs
\> back
\end{tabbing}
