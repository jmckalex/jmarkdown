1. Computability

The modern spelling of the mark and of a subentry, plus a sorted display form whose entry contains brackets — the raw balanced scan has to survive the generic @name[…] directive tokenizer.

A named index still works, and the legacy spelling is still accepted.

1.1. Disambiguation

Marks are glued to the word they index, which is why this is the one @ form that may follow a word character directly.

A bare @index is the placement, not a mark: only the bracket tells them apart. Both forms are shown literally here — @index[entry] and @index{title="…"} — inside code spans, so neither is claimed.
