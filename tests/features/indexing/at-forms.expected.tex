\subsection{Disambiguation}

Marks are glued to the word they index, which is why this is the one \mintinline{text}|@|
form that may follow a word character directly.

A bare \mintinline{text}|@index| is the placement, not a mark: only the bracket tells them
apart. Both forms are shown literally here — \mintinline{text}|@index[entry]| and
\mintinline{text}|@index{title="…"}| — inside code spans, so neither is claimed.
